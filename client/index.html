The Dungeon

A reality show in a dungeon. Chat watches, votes and bets on your death.

Pick your class:

How to play

The goal: survive, get rich and famous, and go as deep as you can. Each floor collapses after 10 minutes (the first one lasts 20). The stairs stay sealed for the first 5 minutes: find the boss room, beat the boss and go down before the timer runs out. Anyone still on the floor when it falls is eliminated, but chat can vote to bring them back.

Turns: every 20 seconds, everyone acts at the same time. You send one command per turn (the last one counts). If you do nothing, you keep attacking your target, or you strike back if someone attacks you. Turn order: potions and items first, then attacks (by initiative: d20 + Dexterity), then everything else. So you can drink before getting hit, but you can't run away before.

Good to know: safe rooms (blue banner) are protected. When you arrive or respawn, you are immune to other players for 3 min, and you can't attack them either. Attacking an ally is a betrayal: big bonus, stolen gold, and everyone will know. When you die, your loot stays on the floor and you come back 5 turns later.

On the map: rooms you've visited keep a colored outline and a crest. Blue: safe room. Gold: forge, or treasure room (dotted). Red: boss room, or trap (solid if armed, dotted if disarmed). Purple: arena. The red dot shows how many monsters you saw last time, and the coin marks loot left on the floor.

Dice: every action = d20 + your stat + half your level. A natural 20 is a crit, a natural 1 is a blooper. Everything shows up in the log.

Mouse and keyboard: WASD (ZQSD on AZERTY) or arrows (or click the floor) to walk around your room; walk into a door to change rooms at the end of the turn; click a monster to attack it. F search, E attack, ? this help. Type without "!" to talk: your message shows up as a speech bubble.
